**Sonic 3 Unlocked** is an extensive modification of *Sonic the Hedgehog 3* which aims to bring over modern sensibilities from the Retro Engine titles back into the Mega Drive original.

**Amy Rose**, **Mighty the Armadillo** and **Ray the Flying Squirrel** join as playable characters, bringing with them their full movesets from Sonic Origins and Sonic Mania.

**Encore Mode** also makes a return from Sonic Mania Plus, allowing you to play a remixed version of the story.

**Many, many bugfixes and quality of life improvements** have been made, with the goal of producing the best version of Sonic 3 available on original hardware.

Finally, various **unlockable extras** are available, with many more planned in the future.

Read on if you want more information on everything that's been added. Enjoy!

# New characters
## Amy Rose
<img align="right" src="assets/char-amy.png"></img>
Amy excels at offense. Her **Hammer Attack** increases her attack radius without increasing her hitbox, but unlike Sonic's insta-shield, Amy does *not* become invincible, so watch out for fire and spikes when attacking bosses!

Hold down the jump button to charge up Amy's **Hammer Rush** ability, which not only makes short work of enemies and breakable walls, but allows her to run up steep slopes with ease.
<br clear="right" />

## Mighty the Armadillo
<img align="right" src="assets/char-mighty.png"></img>
Mighty is a slower, more defensive character. While rolling, his **shell** prevents him from being damaged by spikes, and with enough momentum, allows him to take certain exclusive routes.

His **Hammer Drop** ability allows him to pierce straight through both enemies and breakable floors, opening up a few shortcuts. The impact will also cause any nearby monitors to fall to the ground.
<br clear="right" />

## Ray the Flying Squirrel
<img align="right" src="assets/char-ray.png"></img>
What he lacks in offense and defense, Ray makes up in movement. His **Air Glide** ability allows him to soar freely in the air, and with enough space around him, pick up tremendous speed.

Press the left and right buttons to alternate between gaining altitude and momentum. Release the jump button to revert back into a rolling state, perfect for taking out enemies that jump in your way.
<br clear="right" />

# Encore Mode
<img align="right" src="assets/encore-swap.png"></img>
Encore Mode can be accessed from the title screen. As in Sonic Mania Plus, progress for this mode is saved separately from 1 Player mode.

In Encore Mode, you play as two characters simultaneously, and can change between them at any time. Press the **A button** while both characters are on screen to **swap** which one you're currently controlling.

Swapping may be blocked, for instance, if either character has their controls locked, or if the end of the stage has been reached. In any case though, the character which is currently being controlled by player 1 will briefly be indicated by an arrow over their head.

When player 2 is not on screen, pressing the **A button** will instead **call** them to your position. Player 2 may also manually jump to player 1's position by pressing the **Start button** on the player 2 controller.

## Rescuing characters
<img align="right" src="assets/encore-stocks.png"></img>
Apart from characters who get rescued as part of the story, more characters can be added to your roster by finding special **item boxes** or completing a **Bonus Stage**. Enter a Bonus Stage by activating a star post while carrying 35 or more rings. 

When a character is rescued, they will immediately join the game if nobody else is taking up the player 2 slot. If there are already two characters in play, you will instead be awarded a **stock**, represented by the character's initial on the bottom left portion of the HUD.

Characters rescued as part of the story or by completing a Bonus Stage will immediately join the game and send your current partner character to the end of the **stock queue**. Item boxes will add new characters to your stocks without replacing your partner character.

In Encore Mode, whenever you would normally lose a life, you will instead lose your current character. Control then **swaps** to your partner character, a new partner is pulled from top of the stock queue, and the game continues. If a swap is not possible, the game will instead restart from the last star post, or from the beginning of the stage. Lost characters can be rescued again through normal means.

## Items exclusive to Encore Mode
<img align="right" src="assets/item-roulette.png"></img>
### Roulette
Cycles through all available characters, and awards you whichever one was being displayed when the item box was destroyed. Once there are no more characters left to rescue, Roulette item boxes cycle through a set of normal items instead.
<br clear="right" />

<img align="right" src="assets/item-change.png"></img>
### Change
Sends your current character to the end of the stock queue, and replaces them with the character at the top of the queue. Once there are no more characters left to rescue, this item can also occasionally be found in Bonus Stages.
<br clear="right" />

<img align="right" src="assets/item-combine.png"></img>
### Combine Ring
Combines all of your rings into one. When you take damage, only one ring will fly out. Collect the ring before it explodes, and it will restore all of the rings you just lost.
<br clear="right" />

# New features
<img align="right" src="assets/hud-barrier.png"></img>
**Barrier HUD:** When elemental barriers cannot be displayed, either due to a Super form or an invincibility power-up being active, they are now represented by an icon on the top right corner of the screen. When you have the Combine Ring power-up, it will also appear here.

**Super button:** Super transformations are now bound exclusively to the A button, so you can keep using your character's double jump action without triggering them. The barrier HUD is temporarily replaced with a button prompt for the super transformation when it is available.
<br clear="right" />

<img align="right" src="assets/hud-skip.png"></img>
**Cutscene skips:** Most cutscenes in the game can now be skipped. Whenever the HUD is hidden from view, press the Start button to activate the button prompt, followed by the A button. Score countdowns can also be skipped by pressing the Start button.

**Tails assist:** Double jump moves are now blocked when you hold the Up button and no other directions. This is to facilitate grabbing onto Tails, whose flight can now be controlled by player 1 as in the Marble Garden Zone 2 boss.
<br clear="right" />

<img align="right" src="assets/hud-options.png"></img>
**Hidden abilities:** An extra skill is available for each character, unlockable by clearing 1 Player mode or successfully keeping the character through the end of Encore Mode. Abilities can be toggled via the Options menu, accessed by pressing the A button while in the Data Select menu.

**Erase data menu:** Individual sections of save RAM can be erased by selecting the Delete option in the Data Select menu and then pressing the A button. This can be used, for example, to reset Museum completion data back to its initial state without affecting saved games.
